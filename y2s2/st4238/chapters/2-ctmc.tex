\section{Continuous Time Markov Chain}
A \textbf{\textcolor{Blue}{continuous time Markov chain}} is a
stochastic process $\{X(t), t\geq 0\}$ with a countable state space $S$ s.t.\
for any time points $t_0=0<t_1<t_2<\cdots<t_{n}$ and any states $i_0, i_1, i_2,
\ldots, i_{n}$,
\begin{align*}
  &P\left( X\left( t_{n} \right) =i_{n} | X\left( t_{n-1} \right) =i_{n-1}, \ldots, X\left( t_0 \right) =i_0 \right) \\
  =&P\left( X\left( t_{n} \right) =i_{n} | X\left( t_{n-1} \right) =i_{n-1} \right) 
\end{align*}
