\section{Stat Theorems}
\[E \left\{ E \left[ Y|X \right] \right\}=E \left[ Y \right]\]
\[E\left\{ var\left[ Y|X \right]  \right\} + Var\left( E\left[ Y|X \right]  \right) =Var\left( Y \right).\] 
For a non-negative RV $X$ and $a>0$, by \textbf{Markov's Inequality},
\[P(X\ge a)\le \frac{E(X)}{A}.\]
For RV $X$ with finite $\sigma^{2}\neq 0$ (hence finite $\mu$) and $k>0$, by
\textbf{Chebyshev's Inequality},
\[P\left( \left| X-\mu \right| \ge k\sigma \right) \le \frac{1}{k^{2}}.\] 
For RV $X$ and any convex function $f$ ($f''(x)\ge 0$), by \textbf{Jensen's Inequality},
\[E(f(X))\ge f(E(X)).\] 
with equality only with $g$ is a constant function or affine, or almost surely constant $X$.\\
