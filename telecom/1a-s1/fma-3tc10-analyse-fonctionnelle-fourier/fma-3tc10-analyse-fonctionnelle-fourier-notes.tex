{\parindent0pt \baselineskip=15.5pt
  Functional analysis and Fourier analysis, in the order of the course poly. The
  course sets conventions on the extended real line, then works through the topology
  of normed vector spaces --- metric spaces, continuity of linear and bilinear maps,
  density and separability, completeness and Banach spaces; the Lebesgue integral and
  the $L^p$ spaces, with their inequalities and convolution; Hilbert spaces,
  orthogonal projection, Hilbert bases and Fourier series; the Fourier transform on
  $\mathbb{R}$, first on $L^1$ and then extended to $L^2$; regularity, decay at
  infinity and the Schwartz class; and Fourier analysis on $[0,1[$ and on
  $\mathbb{Z}$, with the Fejér kernel.
